**1. Clear Subject Line**

- This is the first thing that the reader will see, this also gives the reader a quick idea of the content. 
- Priority rating is 5

**2. Use a friendly/professional tone**

- When communicating via email we should always use a friendly and professional tone. Sometimes humour can be taken the wrong way and seen as an insult. So, keep it concise, to the point, and easy to read!
- Priority rating is 3

**3. Concise and Relevant Body**

- This is a key criterion for a good email response because it ensures your message is clear, easy to understand, and respectful of the recipient's time.
- Priority rating is 5

**4. Use correct grammar and spell check**

- Always use the correct grammar within your emails, and spell check at the end. Proofread the whole message before sending it. Sometimes reading an email out loud can help or have someone else check it for you. 
- Priority rating is 4

**5. Personalization**

- If the email sender has given you their first name or title within their message, be sure to use it when greeting them, as this shows attentiveness.
- Priority rating is 4

**6. Closing and Signature**

- End your message with a polite closing to maintain a professional tone.
- Priority rating is 3

**7. Action-Oriented**

- This ensures that the recipient understands what is expected of them and by when. A well-structured email should not leave the reader guessing about follow-up actions. Instead, it should outline any required tasks, assign responsibilities if applicable, and include precise due dates or timeframes.
- Priority rating is 5
